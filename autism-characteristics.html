Autism: Characteristics

Diagnostic criteria and contexts

The DSM-5 (used primarily for a clinical diagnosis) and IDEA (used primarily for an administrative diagnosis) have differing categories for what constitutes an autism diagnosis.

How autism expresses in different contexts

At school

Autistic learners tend to be visual learners and need structured visual supports to navigate transitions and learning opportunities.

At home

Autistic children may have ritualistic or restricted behaviors regarding food, sleep, or social patterns. Families can support these neurodiverse expressions with compassion.

At work

Employers can appreciate autistic employees' strengths by emphasizing attention to detail and special interests.
