Book Reviews

Reading with a pencil

Books reviewed the way a debater reads them: what is the thesis, what is the evidence, and what would the other side say.

  • Disability Visibility (ed. Alice Wong) — reviewed as a virtual diorama: first-person stories, rendered as scenes.
  • A History of Autism — a timeline built from Steve Silberman’s NeuroTribes.
  • God Loves the Autistic Mind (Fr. Matthew Schneider) — prayer, ritual, and the autistic mind.
  • Neuroqueerness as Fugitive Practice (R. Roscigno, 2019) — a respectful disagreement with the fugitivity article, via postmodern critical theory, political theology, and skepticism.
