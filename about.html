About

My name is Gabe Esquivel

I am a master's student at the University of Kansas studying Autism Spectrum Disorder. I received my BA in Economics from the University of Kansas in 2021.

I am currently an English teacher at a cram school in Taiwan, where I have taught for three years. I've used lessons from this degree in my classes to make them more inclusive to learners with autism, primarily by incorporating evidence-based practices.

Our curricular strategy uses the structure of competitive policy and public-forum debate to encourage learners to practice English. Debate gave me my community during my youth, and I hope it can continue to be a community for my students — especially those who can't find it elsewhere.

Many of my students are neurodiverse, and this site is dedicated to sharing what I'm learning about supporting them.
