The first rule

Who has the burden of proof?

The person making the claim carries the burden. When a therapy promises your child recovery, you are not obligated to disprove it — the promiser is obligated to prove it, with evidence that survives scrutiny. Debaters learn this rule first, because without it, the loudest claim wins. Parents deserve the same rule.
